Everything you need to know

Official definitions, formulas, and worked examples for all 11 Cambodia tax types.

Salary Tax
Tax on Salary (TOS) — GDT
TOS
Monthly salary tax accepts KHR or USD salary. USD salary is converted at 1 USD = 4,000 KHR, then resident deductions or non-resident flat tax rules are applied.
Currency type: KHR or USD Monthly gross salary: entered value Exchange rate: 1 USD = 4,000 KHR Resident employees: Each dependent child: 150,000 KHR Unemployed spouse: 150,000 KHR 0 - 1,500,000 KHR → 0 1,500,001 - 2,000,000 → Income x 5% - 75,000 2,000,001 - 8,500,000 → Income x 10% - 175,000 8,500,001 - 12,500,000 → Income x 15% - 600,000 > 12,500,000 → Income x 20% - 1,225,000 Non-resident employees: Salary tax = Taxable income x 20%
Example: Resident gross salary KHR 4,800,000 with unemployed spouse and 2 children → taxable income KHR 4,350,000 → tax = KHR 260,000.
Value Added Tax
VAT — General Department of Taxation
VAT
VAT can be calculated from a price excluding VAT, reversed from a VAT-included price, or calculated for Cambodia imports using CIF, customs duty, and optional specific tax.
Exchange rate: 1 USD = 4,000 KHR VAT : 10% Basic VAT (Price Excluded VAT) VAT = Price × 10% Total = Price + VAT Reverse VAT (Price Included VAT) Base price = Price ÷ 1.1 VAT = Price − Base price Import VAT (CIF-based - Cambodia) 0% Raw materials / special goods 7% Basic materials 15% Intermediate goods 35% Luxury / finished goods Customs Duty = CIF × Duty Rate Specific Tax = CIF × Specific Tax Rate Tax Base = CIF + Customs Duty + Specific Tax VAT = Tax Base × 10% Total Pay = Tax Base + VAT
Example: Price USD 100 without VAT → VAT USD 10.00 → Total USD 110.00.
Prepayment Tax
Monthly tax on revenue
PTOI
Prepayment Tax is calculated by applying 1% to monthly revenue.
Prepayment Tax = Monthly Revenue × 1%
Example: Monthly revenue KHR 40,000,000 → Prepayment Tax = KHR 400,000.
Withholding Tax
WHT — Tax withheld at source
WHT
Deducted at the time of payment. Rates depend on payment type, recipient residency, bank interest type, and the self-declaration service exemption.
Resident services / royalties / interest: 15% Resident asset sale: 10% Bank fixed deposit: 6% Bank regular savings: 4% Non-resident payment: 14% Self-declaration services: 0%
Example: Resident royalty payment KHR 10,000,000 → WHT = KHR 1,500,000.
Minimum Tax
MT — Floor tax on turnover
MT
Minimum Tax is calculated by applying 1% to annual revenue.
Minimum Tax = Annual Revenue × 1%
Example: Annual revenue KHR 100,000,000 → Minimum Tax = KHR 1,000,000.
Income Tax
Annual tax for companies, insurance, and individuals
INC
Annual Income Tax has three cases: flat corporate tax for big companies, special rates for insurance companies, and progressive annual brackets for individuals or small businesses.
Standard company: 20% Oil/Gas/Mining: 30% QIP: 0% / special exemption General insurance: 5% Life insurance: 20% Individuals: 0% - 20% progressive
Example: Individual income KHR 120,000,000 → Income Tax = KHR 10,800,000.
Patent Tax
Annual business registration tax
PAT
Annual business registration tax. The calculator applies fixed fees by taxpayer size and multiplies by required registrations for activities and branch locations.
Small taxpayer: KHR 400,000 Medium taxpayer: KHR 1,200,000 Large taxpayer: KHR 3,000,000 Large high-turnover taxpayer: KHR 5,000,000 Total Patent Tax = Base Tax × Registrations
Example: Medium taxpayer with 2 required registrations → Patent Tax = KHR 2,400,000.
Property Tax
TOIP, unused land, and rental tax
PROP
Property Tax is calculated by selecting Tax on Immovable Property, Unused Land Tax, or Rental Tax.
Exchange rate: 1 USD = 4,000 KHR 1. Tax on Immovable Property (TOIP) Property Tax= [(Tax Base × 80%)-25000$]x 0.1% 2. Unused Land Tax Tax = Tax Base × 2% 3. Rental Tax Total Rent = Monthly total rent amount Resident Tax = Total Rent × 10% Non-resident Tax = Total Rent × 14%
Example: select the property tax type, enter the tax base or rental details, then calculate the tax payable.
Specific Tax
Selective ad valorem tax on goods and services
ST
Specific Tax applies to selected goods and services, including listed goods categories and service categories with their own SPT rates.
Exchange rate: 1 USD = 4,000 KHR Goods (Specific Tax) Product SPT Rate Alcohol / Spirits 35% Beer 30% Cigarettes 20% Cigars 25% Beverages / Soft Drinks 10% Cement 5% Formula Tax Base Tax Base = 0.9 × ((Invoice Price ÷ 1.1) ÷ (1 + SPT Rate)) SPT Amount SPT Amount = Tax Base × SPT Rate Services (Specific Tax) Service SPT Rate Air Passenger Transport 10% Entertainment Services 10% Telecommunications 3% Formula Tax Base Tax Base = Invoice Price ÷ (1 + 10% + SPT Rate) SPT Amount SPT Amount = Tax Base × SPT Rate
Example: Beer invoice price USD 10,000.00 → Tax base USD 6,293.71 → Specific Tax at 30% = USD 1,888.11.
Accommodation Tax
Tax on accommodation services
AT
Accommodation Tax is imposed at a rate of 2% on accommodation services provided in Cambodia. The calculator supports VAT-exclusive and VAT-inclusive invoice prices.
Exchange rate: 1 USD = 4,000 KHR Accommodation Tax Rate: 2% Excluding VAT Formula: Accommodation Tax = Invoice Price × 2% Including VAT Formula: Tax Base = Invoice Price ÷ 1.12 Accommodation Tax = Tax Base × 2%
Examples: excluding VAT, USD 100 invoice → USD 2 tax. Including VAT, USD 112 invoice → USD 100 tax base → USD 2 tax.
Transfer Tax
Land concession, family transfer, and buy/sell
TRF
Transfer Tax is calculated by selecting the transfer type, then choosing the relationship or transfer share when required.
Land Concession: 0% General property transfer: Tax Base × 4% First-time Transfer: Direct family + full ownership transfer = 0% Gift, non-direct family = (Tax Base × 4%) - 4,000,000 KHR Inheritance, non-direct family = (Tax Base × 4%) - 8,000,000 KHR Second-time Transfer: Gift within family circle from the 2nd time onward, including partial ownership Tax = (Tax Base × Transfer Share × 4%) - 4,000,000 KHR Buy / Sell: Tax Base × 4%
Example: second-time transfer of 50% share → transfer tax = (tax base × 50% × 4%) - 4,000,000 KHR.
