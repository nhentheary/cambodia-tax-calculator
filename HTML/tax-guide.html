All Cambodia taxes
you need to know

Based on GDT Law on Taxation (LoT). Click any card to learn more.

💰
Salary Tax
0% – 20% progressive
Withheld monthly by employers. Resident uses KHR brackets; non-resident flat 20%.
🧾
Value Added Tax
10% / 0% exports
Output VAT on sales offset by input VAT on purchases; exports are zero-rated.
📊
Prepayment Tax
1% monthly revenue
Calculated by applying 1% to monthly revenue.
✂️
Withholding Tax (WHT)
14% – 15% by type
Deducted at source. Covers interest, royalties, dividends, non-resident services.
📉
Minimum Tax
1% of annual turnover
Applies when annual Tax on Income is less than 1% of turnover. Ensures a floor tax payment.
💼
Income Tax
0% - 30%
Annual tax for big companies, insurance companies, and individuals or small businesses.
📜
Patent Tax
Fixed annual fee
Annual business registration tax paid by all enterprises operating in Cambodia.
🏠
Property Tax
0.1% of assessed value
Annual tax on immovable property valued over 100 million KHR.
🏭
Specific Tax
Varies by product
Selective ad valorem tax on vehicles, tobacco, alcohol, air tickets, entertainment, telecom, green technology, and more.
🤝
Transfer Tax
4% of transfer value
Tax on transfer of immovable property ownership. Paid by the buyer.
🏨
Accommodation Tax
2% of revenue
Tax on accommodation services provided by hotels, guesthouses, resorts, serviced apartments, and similar lodging establishments.
