Tax description

Accommodation Tax

Accommodation Tax is imposed at a rate of 2% on accommodation services provided in Cambodia. The calculator supports both VAT-exclusive and VAT-inclusive invoice prices.

Exchange Rate

1 USD = 4,000 KHR

Formula

Excluding VAT
Accommodation Tax = Invoice Price × 2%

Including VAT
Tax Base = Invoice Price ÷ 1.12
Accommodation Tax = Tax Base × 2%

Notes and Conditions

When the invoice price includes VAT (10%) and Accommodation Tax (2%), extract the tax base first by dividing the invoice price by 1.12.
