Salary Tax
Tax on Salary (TOS) — GDT
TOS
Monthly salary tax applies differently for resident and non-resident employees. Salary can be entered in KHR or USD; USD salary is converted at the fixed exchange rate of 1 USD = 4,000 KHR before tax is calculated.
Currency type: KHR or USD Monthly gross salary: entered value Exchange rate: 1 USD = 4,000 KHR Resident employees: Each dependent child: 150,000 KHR Unemployed spouse: 150,000 KHR Taxable income = Gross salary + Taxable benefits - Exempt allowances - Dependent deductions 0 - 1,500,000 KHR → 0 1,500,001 - 2,000,000 → Income x 5% - 75,000 2,000,001 - 8,500,000 → Income x 10% - 175,000 8,500,001 - 12,500,000 → Income x 15% - 600,000 > 12,500,000 → Income x 20% - 1,225,000 Non-resident employees: Taxable income = Gross salary + Taxable benefits - Exempt allowances Salary tax = Taxable income x 20%
Example: Resident gross salary KHR 4,800,000 with unemployed spouse and 2 children → Deductions KHR 450,000 → Taxable income KHR 4,350,000 → Tax KHR 260,000 → Net salary KHR 4,540,000.
